The problem

Healthcare information is everywhere. Clarity isn't.

Most people don't struggle because health information doesn't exist — they struggle because it's scattered, inconsistent, and hard to act on.

01

Confusing information

Health content online is often contradictory, overly technical, or written for clinicians rather than patients.

02

Uncertainty about care

People often don't know whether a concern needs urgent attention, a specialist, or simply time and rest.

03

Unreliable resources

Search results mix credible medical sources with unverified claims, making it hard to know what to trust.

04

Fragmented discovery

Finding the right doctor or pharmacy usually means jumping between apps, directories, and word of mouth.

Our solution

An assistance layer between a health question and the right next step.

Pulse AI doesn't replace healthcare professionals — it helps people move from confusion to clarity, and from clarity to appropriate care.

A

Ask

Describe a health question or concern in plain language.

G

C

Get pointed toward the appropriate professional or service.
